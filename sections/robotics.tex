\section{Robotics and Control Projects}
\project{Wheeled-Bipedal Robot Modeling and Control}{KAIST \enspace$\vert$\enspace MATLAB/Simulink, CasADi, C++, Gazebo}{Sep.\ 2024 -- Oct.\ 2025}
\cvItemsStart
\item Modeled the robot as a wheeled inverted pendulum and formulated velocity-tracking optimization with soft Lyapunov constraints and state and motor-torque limits.
\item Built a C++ interface between Gazebo and Simulink to exchange joint states, IMU measurements, and torque commands for controller development in simulation.
\cvItemsEnd
\project{Risk-Sensitive Stochastic Control (RS-JiLQG)}{KAIST graduate course project \enspace$\vert$\enspace Python, NumPy}{Sep.\ 2024 -- Oct.\ 2025}
\cvItemsStart
\item Co-developed a risk-sensitive iterative LQG formulation to control nonlinear systems under Gaussian noise and sudden wheel-slip disturbances.
\item Implemented the solver and evaluated risk settings through Monte Carlo simulations of a wheeled inverted pendulum, comparing mean and entropic costs.
\cvItemsEnd
