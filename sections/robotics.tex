\project{Wheeled-Bipedal Robot Modeling and Control}{KAIST \enspace$\vert$\enspace MATLAB/Simulink, CasADi, C++, Gazebo}{Sep.\ 2024 -- Oct.\ 2025}
\cvItemsStart
\item Modeled the robot as a wheeled inverted pendulum and formulated a control-Lyapunov-function MPC in CasADi for velocity tracking under state and torque limits, with polynomial reference trajectories.
\item Wrote a C++ Gazebo plugin with Protocol Buffers messages that exchanges joint states, IMU data, and torque commands with Simulink for co-simulation.

\project{Risk-Sensitive Stochastic Control (RS-JiLQG)}{KAIST graduate course project (EE899), with a classmate \enspace$\vert$\enspace Python, NumPy}{Fall 2025}
\cvItemsStart
\item Derived a risk-sensitive iterative LQG for nonlinear jump-diffusion systems: an entropic-risk objective with Bernoulli-approximated slip jumps and a jump-augmented backward pass.
\item Built a jump-diffusion simulator of a wheeled inverted pendulum with EKF/UKF state estimation; compared mean and entropic costs across risk levels and against iLQG and MPC in Monte Carlo rollouts.
\cvItemsEnd
