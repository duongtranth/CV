\subproject{BeliefCO \enspace{\normalfont\small\textit{Master's thesis, ongoing}}}
\textit{End-to-End Transformer-Based Multi-Object Tracking for Drone and Bird Surveillance}
\cvItemsStart
\item Built a belief into the CO-MOT tracker: per-track Gaussian motion hypotheses and existence probabilities drive a Poisson multi-Bernoulli association solved by in-network belief propagation.
\item Added track dropout, camera-motion compensation, and a query-embedding appearance likelihood.
\item Created WOSDETC tracking annotations (birds and drones, CVAT) and recording-level splits; evaluated on BFT, WOSDETC, BSB, and SMOT4SB with every baseline on the same detector.
\item Achieved test HOTA of \textbf{72.32 on BFT}, \textbf{62.91 on WOSDETC}, and \textbf{65.77 on BSB}, against 69.72, 59.44, and 63.81 for the strongest baselines, and the highest IDF1 on all four test benchmarks.
\cvItemsEnd
